We have redesigned our _Emerging Pathogens_ section as _Outbreaks_ section to remain time relevant and accessible. We have updated the layout of pages to improve readability, navigation and presentation of outbreak related data and background. The new format is designed to give you a clearer overview of key information at a glance, while also making deeper pathogen and outbreak details easier to access.

### What’s new

- **Streamlined section** - With the background section, It is now easier to navigate and understand the pathogen’s biology and transmission routes.
- **Concise details** - The outbreak summary includes confirmed case numbers, timeline of public notices, and status of investigations.
- **Consistent layout across pathogens** - Similar structure for all outbreak entries under Outbreaks section, making comparisons and navigation simpler.
- **Improved linking and references** - direct links to national health‑agency pages and source documents, plus clearer citations and data provenance.  

We believe this redesign will support researchers, public‑health professionals and the general public in accessing outbreak information with greater clarity and speed. As always, the Portal remains committed to providing up‑to‑date, accurate information on emerging pathogens and ongoing outbreaks.
