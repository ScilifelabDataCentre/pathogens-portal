Several academic Biosafety Level 3 (BSL3) facilities within the Swedish BSL3 Network are prepared to support research on highly pathogenic hantaviruses. This includes Andes virus, which was linked to the recent international outbreak. The network provides national readiness and infrastructure capacity for safe handling and research of these agents. More information is available on the [Swedish BSL3 Network page](https://ki.se/en/research/research-infrastructure-and-environments/core-facilities-for-research/biosafety-and-translational-facilities/swedish-bsl3-network).

For more information, contact the network directly: [bsl3-network@ki.se](mailto:bsl3-network@ki.se).

Information about the hantavirus outbreak is available from multiple sources, please refer to our recent [hantavirus outbreak news article](/updates/hantavirus_outbreak) to access them. The Swedish Pathogens Portal continues to monitor outbreaks that impact Sweden, and will always endeavour to make any research resources available as soon as possible. Please [contact us](/contact) with any requests.
