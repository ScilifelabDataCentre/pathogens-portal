The latest editorial, written by [Eva Garmendia](https://www.uu.se/en/contact-and-organisation/staff?query=N12-1889) (Project Coordinator at the [Uppsala Antibiotic Center](http://www.uac.uu.se/), Uppsala University), is entitled “[_Antibiotic resistance does not recognize disciplinary borders: The Uppsala Antibiotic Center’s approach to research and education_](editorials/uppsala_antibiotic_center)”. It discusses antibiotic resistance is a complex, borderless challenge that cannot be addressed within disciplinary experts alone. It argues that meaningful progress depends not only on scientific advances, but on sustained collaboration across clinical, technical, social, and environmental domains.

This editorial focuses on the approach at the [Uppsala Antibiotic Center](http://www.uac.uu.se/) (UAC) to create a sustainable community working towards addressing complex antibiotic resistance needs. UAC positions itself as a shared space where diverse expertise converges to ask broader, more impactful questions. Education is framed as critical infrastructure, equipping early-career researchers with systems thinking and cross-disciplinary fluency. Ultimately, the intent is to cultivate a durable, intellectually open community capable of managing antibiotic resistance through collective responsibility and integrated knowledge.

The [Uppsala Antibiotic Days](https://bit.ly/UppsalaAntibioticDays) (bi-annual international conference) happening on 15-16 June 2026 organized by UAC brings together researchers, clinicians, policymakers, and industry across career stages. It offers time and space for dialogue, reflection, and new connections. If you work with antibiotic resistance and are looking for a community that grows with you, this is an invitation. The author encourages you to take part, to contribute, and to help shape how the field moves forward.
