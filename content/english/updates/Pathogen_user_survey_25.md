---
title: "Survey to improve the Swedish Pathogens Portal"
date: 2025-09-08
summary: The Swedish Pathogens Portal is seeking feedback from users to identify how it can be improved to better meet their needs. Please complete the survey to share your thoughts!
---

The Swedish Pathogens Portal is a community-driven portal. As such, it continuously adapts to best serve the needs of its community. To be able to do this effectively, we're seeking your feedback on what you need from the Swedish Pathogens Portal, and what we can do to improve it, so that it remains a valuable resource.```

**Please fill in our latest [user survey](https://scilifelab.typeform.com/data-consumer) to provide your thoughts by 30th September.**

This survey seeks to determine;

(1) What kinds of infectious disease/pandemic preparedness data are currently most important to the community.

(2) What more can be done to support data sharing, or improve access to data.

(3) What features could be addded or modified to make the portal a more valuable resource for the community.

Don't forget that the **[Pathogen Resource Proposal call](https://anubis.scilifelab.se/call/PATHOGENS2025) is still open until 19th September**. The call seeks proposals for new resources that will be beneficial to the Swedish research community.
