The Pandemic Laboratory Preparedness (PLP) program is a major part of SciLifeLab's pandemic preparedness efforts. It supports the development of [capabilities](/resources/) that support and complement the pandemic preparedness efforts of Sweden's authorities, municipalities, and regions, among others.

In June 2025, the PLP program launched a new funding call (PLPTDP25) for Technology Development Projects (TDPs). The aim of this call was to ensure that the country can detect, analyse, and respond to emerging infectious threats rapidly and effectively by investing in tools and systems that are scalable, sustainable, and ready for real-world deployment. Areas of particular interest were; diagnostics and analyses of infectious diseases, analyses of immune responses during infections and vaccination, and studies of drug resistance development in infectious agents.

Twelve capabilities were funded as a result of this call, information about these capabilities is now available in the [PLP capabilities](/resources/) section. These capabilities will be funded until at least September 2027.
