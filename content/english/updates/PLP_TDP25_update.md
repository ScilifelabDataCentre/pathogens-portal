The PLP program is a major part of SciLifeLab's pandemic preparedness efforts. The program supports the development of [capabilities](/resources/) that support and complement the pandemic preparedness efforts of authorities, municipalities, and regions, among others.

In June 2025, Pandemic Laboratory Preparedness (PLP) programme launched a funding call—PLPTDP25—for Technology Development Projects (TDPs). This call was designed to drive rapid, large-scale diagnostics and host response analysis amid outbreaks by funding innovation and refinement of technologies and capabilities that will significantly strengthen Sweden’s national pandemic preparedness. The aim was to ensure that the country can detect, analyse, and respond to emerging infectious threats rapidly and effectively by investing in tools and systems that are scalable, sustainable, and ready for real-world deployment.

Information about the capabilities developed during this call is now available in our [capabilities](/resources/) section.
