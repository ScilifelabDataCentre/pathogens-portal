We are delighted to announce the launch of the [Chilean Pathogens Portal](https://patogenos.cl/), a new national Pathogen Portal Node (PPN) developed to support pathogen surveillance, infectious disease research, and FAIR data sharing in Chile. Like other national Pathogen Portal Nodes (PPNs), the [Chilean Pathogens Portal](https://patogenos.cl/) has been developed using the [Pathogen Portal Node (PPN) Toolbox] (https://github.com/ScilifelabDataCentre/node-pathogens-portal), an open framework created through the [Pathogen Data Network (PDN)](https://pathogendatanetwork.org/) funded by NIH (award number U24AI183840). The toolbox enables countries and organisations to establish interoperable national portals while maintaining local ownership of their data, resources, and priorities.

The portal provides a central access point to information on priority pathogens affecting Chile. It brings together interactive dashboards, outbreak updates, surveillance summaries, and curated resources to support researchers, public health professionals, clinicians, and policymakers. The portal covers a broad range of pathogens, including respiratory viruses, vector-borne diseases, antimicrobial resistance, foodborne pathogens, and emerging infectious diseases, with data linked to official national and international sources.

The portal has been developed through a collaboration involving [Universidad de Chile](https://uchile.cl/), the [Advanced Center for Chronic Diseases (ACCDiS)](https://accdis.cl/), [CHAIR – Center for HIV/AIDS Integral Research](https://medicina.uchile.cl/investigacion/centros-de-la-facultad/centro-de-investigacion-integral-en-vihsida-chair-), and the [Unidad de Genómica Avanzada (UGA)](https://quimica.uchile.cl/facultad/laboratorio-de-vinculacion-y-centro-de-servicios/unidad-de-genomica-avanzada-uga), with support from the Pathogen Data Network (PDN), Chile's [FONDAP programme](https://www.conicyt.cl/fondap/fondap-program/), and the [ICAI Platform](https://uchile.cl/investigacion/programas-y-proyectos-institucionales/plataforma-icai-de-la-universidad-de-chile).

The launch of the [Chilean Pathogens Portal](https://patogenos.cl/) marks another important milestone in the growing international ecosystem of national pathogen portals. Together, these portals improve the visibility, accessibility, and interoperability of pathogen-related data and resources, strengthening global preparedness and collaboration for infectious disease surveillance and research.

We congratulate the Chilean team on this achievement and look forward to continued collaboration through the [Pathogen Data Network (PDN)](https://pathogendatanetwork.org/).

To keep up to date with the growing network of Pathogens Portals, check out our [national portals page](/other_national_portals).
