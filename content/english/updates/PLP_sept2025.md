The PLP program is a major part of SciLifeLab's pandemic preparedness efforts. The program supports the development of [capabilities](/resources/) that support and complement the pandemic preparedness efforts of authorities, municipalities, and regions, among others. Many of these capabilities have recently reported their findings, as well as their impact on future pandemic preparedness. Check out the individual [capabilities](/resources/) to read more.

The latest PLP Network meeting took place at the end of August. At this meeting, the network discussed the current and future activities of the PLP Program. Summarised information about the outcomes of the meeting is available [in a news item on scilifelab.se](https://www.scilifelab.se/news/pandemic-laboratory-preparedness-network-meeting-2025/). Information about recent calls from the PLP Program is also available on our [PLP background page](/plp-program-background/).
